# Normal-stock coverage audit, October 2026

Scope: every enabled non-bank stock of the stklive universe (Primary and Secondary tier). Banks and equity
certificates have their own scorecard and are not covered here. Generated by
`scripts/stock_coverage_audit.py` (`--state before` froze the starting point, `--state after` is the current state).

Notes on reading the tables:

* Coverage is the share of the configured evidence weight (`STOCK_EVIDENCE_WEIGHTS`) that has a usable component.
* "Fundamental" rows show value, basis (TTM = four known quarters, FY = last fiscal year, latest balance, spot),
  the as-of period end, the source and, when empty, the reason.
* All values are as known at the decision time; a fact fetched later never enters an earlier view.

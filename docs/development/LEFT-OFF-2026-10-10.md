  - Most failures are tests that target pre-revamp page internals: attributes that were removed, old page structure, and `PageView.controls`.
  - Some failures are real regressions where the revamp dropped a feature: the instrument-detail valuation workspace, "Score-component metric history", the selected journal, operations records, and the risk `SCOREBOARD_PATH`.
  - The `refactor_parity` goldens differ because of intended scoring changes. Regenerate them only with that attribution (relative-strength anchor VWCE with an as-of join; forecast weights 0, so forecasts are separate from scores; Sparebank scorecard v1.2.x): set `ETF_REFRESH_REFACTOR_GOLDENS=1`, then rerun without it.

- `tests/bughunt/test_repro_s3.py` can hang, so run it alone with a timeout.
- Rules for fixing: never weaken, skip or delete a test. Retarget a test only when the behaviour it guards still exists. Restore dropped features.

## Known bugs, highest score impact first
1. **UK stock fundamentals have a GBp/GBP unit error** (BA.L, RR.L, CRN.L). Market cap is about 100× too low, P/E shows 0.3x, FCF yield 432%, and "Stock value 10/10" inflates the score. Prices were fixed by `_minor_unit_divisor` in yfinance; the fundamentals path needs the same scaling. See click-test-3 #29.
2. Sparebank open review findings (`rc-2026-10-10/sol-review-sbfix.txt` #2-#7):
   - Sustainable ROE can never resolve, because securities and rate-cycle history are always listed as missing. The owner-valuation axis is therefore unavailable, which is fail-closed.
   - The legacy reverse-valuation branch bypasses the normalised assumptions.
   - `_filing_identity` accepts `filing_version` alone.
   - Statement incompatibility can lose its reasons.
   - A missing owner earnings figure returns `normalised={}` without a reason.
   - History is sorted by run time, not by reporting period.
3. **ESEF EC pool-fact extraction is incomplete.** Re-importing NONG's official 2024 package gives only `utjevningsfond` and `sparebankens_fond`; `ec_capital`, `overkursfond` and `registered_ec_count` are missing. Arelle logs "document not successfully loaded" for the .xbri and .zip files. The test data copies hold richer evidence from an earlier build. A fresh install or next year's filings will lose valuation coverage, dropping from 0.77 to 0.40. Fix this in `scripts/import_official_filing.py` and `src/etf_cockpit/data/esef_extensions.py`.
4. **Cross-page values differ.** Quality differs between Home/Scores and the Screener (XAIX 5.2 vs 5.9). Quality is a constant 5.8 across stocks. "Evidence confidence 0.0" shows next to a valid score. Components disagree: "7 of 11" vs "9 valid".
5. **Stock Research verdict for Sparebanks** shows "Pending / Fails 5 of 9 gates" despite a native score of 8.6.
6. **Sparebank axes header** ("Axes without evidence…") contradicts the rated rows; it lists axes that show a rating.
7. **Search** does not find Yahoo symbols (BA.L, RABO.AS), and Enter on a page name does nothing.
8. **Sectors & Countries** has no ETF look-through: 76% is "Unknown", and there are duplicate buckets ("financials" and "Financials"). The VWCE sector split is 95.8% "Other".
9. **Raw text leaks to the UI**:
   - "RuntimeError: yfinance is not installed" in alerts and Errors.
   - Raw dumps in the History tab.
   - Raw URLs and ISO timestamps in the fund view.
   - Jargon reasons ("financial_decision_time_unavailable").
10. **Slowness**: cold Home takes 27–90 s with a white screen, and most navigation takes 5–13 s.
11. **Data & Models** cards don't navigate. The Forecast Lab status is stale after a run. Settings chips are clipped. Label overlap on the Scores header, sunburst and radar.
12. **RABO.AS** (Rabobank certificates) has no Yahoo price history, so it is shown unscored with that reason. Plain certificates no longer route to the Sparebank scorecard.

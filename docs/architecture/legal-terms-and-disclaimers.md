# Legal terms and disclaimers

`configs/legal_terms_registry.yaml` is the local, versioned record of source,
model and package terms used by the application. It is a governance record,
not legal advice and not a substitute for professional review.

Mandatory workflows use local user-owned files or explicitly cached official
evidence. Optional providers, model weights and provider feeds are visible but
remain non-blocking. A restricted source is not copied into a standard audit
export; metadata and provenance may be retained where the registry allows it.

The registry records the permitted cache, redistribution and audit-export
behaviour for every source. A missing or unresolved mandatory entry fails the
validator. A terms change requires a new checksum and review; unclear terms
disable an optional capability and block release until resolved.

The application wording is deliberately bounded: research and education only;
not financial or tax advice; no broker execution or order transmission. The
jurisdiction rows remain `review_required` until professional review has been
completed for the intended release jurisdictions.

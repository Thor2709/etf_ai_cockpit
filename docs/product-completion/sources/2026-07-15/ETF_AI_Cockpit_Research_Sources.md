# ETF AI Cockpit — Research Source Register

- **Repository — ETF AI Cockpit current repository**  
  https://github.com/Thor2709/etf_ai_cockpit  
  Use: Primary source for current code, plan and canonical issue ledger.  
  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — SEC EDGAR APIs and nightly bulk archives**  
  https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces  
  Use: Official US submissions and XBRL facts; bulk-first, fair-access ingestion.  
  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — SEC Form N-PORT data sets**  
  https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-n-port-data-sets  
  Use: Quarterly public US registered-fund portfolio data sets.  
  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — Companies House free company data product**  
  https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html  
  Use: Free monthly UK company snapshot for entity, status and SIC context.  
  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — Companies House free accounts data product**  
  https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_accountsdata.html

  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — Eurostat API data access**  
  https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-introduction

  Quality: Primary / High

- **Official data — World Bank Indicators API**

- **Research governance — Harvey, Liu and Zhu — …and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns**  
  https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhv059

  https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15365

  Quality: Working paper / Moderate

- **Research governance — Bailey et al. — The Probability of Backtest Overfitting**  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253

  Quality: Methodological paper / Moderate–High

- **Research governance — Bailey and López de Prado — The Deflated Sharpe Ratio**  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551

  Quality: Methodological paper / Moderate–High

- **Risk research — Ledoit and Wolf — Honey, I Shrunk the Sample Covariance Matrix**  
  https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2004.110

- **Portfolio research — DeMiguel, Garlappi and Uppal — Optimal Versus Naive Diversification**
